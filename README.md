# BlinkIt-vs-Zepto-Dashboard
A Power BI dashboard comparing Zepto vs Blinkit grocery sales. It highlights total sales, delivery time, ratings, payment modes, and top-performing products. Insights reveal mid-year sales peaks, UPI as the most used payment method, and Chennai leading in total revenue.

# 🛒 Grocery Store Sales Dashboard | Zepto vs Blinkit

## 📘 Overview  
This Power BI dashboard provides a comparative analysis of **Zepto and Blinkit’s** grocery sales performance.  
It focuses on **total sales, delivery efficiency, customer ratings, payment preferences, and product categories**, helping identify which brand performs better across key metrics.

---

## 🔑 Key Highlights
- **Total Sales:** 664K  
- **Average Delivery Time:** 15.49 minutes  
- **Average Rating:** 2.99 ⭐  

---

## 📆 Monthly Sales Trend
- Peak month: **June (107K)** followed by **May (86K)** and **July (89K)**.  
- Sales dip during **October and November (25K)**, indicating possible off-season or operational slowdown.  
- Consistent performance between **April–July**, showing strong mid-year sales growth.  

---

## 💳 Payment Mode Insights
- **UPI:** 239K (35.9%) – most preferred payment method  
- **COD:** 218K (32.8%)  
- **Card:** 208K (31.2%)  
This shows a balanced distribution of payment choices, with a strong lean toward **digital payments (UPI)**.  

---

## 🧃 Product Performance
Top-selling products include:  
- **Snacks, Milk, and Paneer** (70K each)  
- **Rice, Coke, Biscuits, and Eggs** also contribute significantly.  
- Lower-performing items include **Bread, Butter, and Atta** (around 50K).  

This indicates that **snack and dairy items** drive the highest sales volumes, reflecting customer preference for quick-consumption goods.

---

## 🗓️ Quarterly & Category Overview
- The dashboard allows filtering by **Quarter (Q1–Q4)** and **Category (Bakery, Grains, Beverages, Poultry, Dairy, Snacks)**.  
- This enables a deeper look at which product types perform best in specific quarters.  

---

## 🏙️ Regional Performance
City-wise sales distribution shows:  
- **Chennai:** 150K  
- **Mumbai:** 141K  
- **Bangalore:** 129K  
- **Hyderabad:** 128K  
- **Delhi:** 116K  

Chennai leads the market, indicating strong brand presence and consumer loyalty, while Delhi trails slightly, suggesting potential for growth.

---

## 📊 Insights Summary
- Blinkit and Zepto maintain **close competition** across all metrics.  
- **Mid-year months (April–July)** are peak sales periods for both.  
- **Digital payments (UPI)** dominate the transaction share.  
- **Dairy and snack categories** remain top revenue contributors.  
- **Chennai** emerges as the highest-performing region in total sales.

---

## 🎯 Conclusion
This dashboard helps stakeholders:  
- Compare **brand performance (Zepto vs Blinkit)**  
- Analyze **seasonal sales trends**  
- Identify **top-selling products** and **preferred payment methods**  
- Strategize marketing efforts by **region and category**
